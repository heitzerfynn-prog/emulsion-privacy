GAUGE · PRIVACY & SUPPORT

Privacy.
Your footage stays on your phone.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

No data collection

Gauge does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and the app never connects to a server.

Camera, microphone and photos

The camera and microphone are only used while you film inside Gauge. Clips you import or record, your looks and your LUTs are stored only on your device. Finished films are saved to your photo library only when you tap “Save to Photos”. Everything is removed when you delete the app.

DATENSCHUTZ

Gauge erhebt, speichert oder überträgt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Es gibt keine Konten, keine Werbung, kein Tracking und keine Serververbindung.
